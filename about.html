About Kaywood.

Kaywood Capital is a Web3 fund based in Lisbon. We build the layer where real-world assets meet on-chain capital, and we invest across digital assets with conviction, connecting Africa, the Gulf and the Lusophone world.

// north_star

Own the layer, not the labour.

The biggest value in any system sits with whoever owns the layer everything else runs on. We focus on those layers: the tokens that carry ownership, the rails that move money, and the relationships that open markets. We leave the busywork to others.
