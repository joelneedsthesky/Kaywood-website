// tokenization

Real assets on-chain, done properly.

Real-world asset (RWA) tokenization turns ownership of property, company shares, credit or intellectual property into digital tokens and NFTs that can be issued, held and transferred on a blockchain. Minting the token is the easy part. We focus on the hard part: legal structure, compliance and real investor demand.

Real estate

Residential and commercial property in gateway cities, split into fractions for a wider investor base.

Equity & private markets

Shares in private companies and funds, with transfer rules written into the token.

Credit & yield

Trade finance, infrastructure debt and tokenized treasuries with on-chain income.

Digital IP & collectibles

NFTs

NFTs backed by real rights such as royalties, licences or provenance, approached selectively.

how_it_works

  1. 01 — OriginateSource the asset and complete legal, valuation and title diligence.
  2. 02 — StructureHold it in an SPV or fund wrapper, backed by a legal opinion.
  3. 03 — IssueMint permissioned tokens that only whitelisted investors can hold.
  4. 04 — DistributeOnboard investors through the portal, with secondary trading where permitted.
  • KYC / AML on every holder
  • Transfer rules enforced on-chain
  • Regulated custody · [partner]
  • EU-aligned · MiFID II · DLT Pilot Regime · MiCA
